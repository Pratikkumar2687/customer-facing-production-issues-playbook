# Customer-Facing Incident Communication

## Overview

Clear communication is an important part of incident management. Customers should receive accurate information without unnecessary internal technical details.

---

## Communication Principles

Communication should be:

- Clear
- Concise
- Accurate
- Professional
- Timely

---

## Initial Communication

Example:

> We are investigating an issue affecting [functionality]. Our engineering team is actively working to identify the cause and restore normal service.

---

## Progress Update

Example:

> Our engineering team has identified the issue and is working on a resolution. We are continuing to monitor the affected functionality.

---

## Resolution Update

Example:

> The issue affecting [functionality] has been resolved. We have verified that the service is operating normally and will continue monitoring it.

---

## Avoid

Do not communicate:

- Unconfirmed root causes
- Internal credentials
- Customer PII
- Internal architecture details
- Blame
- Speculation

---

## Internal vs. Customer Communication

**Internal:**

> Database connection pool exhaustion caused request failures.

**Customer-facing:**

> Customers experienced intermittent failures while using the affected functionality.

The level of technical detail should match the audience.
