# Customer Impact Assessment

## Overview

Customer impact assessment determines how a technical incident affects users and business operations. Technical severity and customer impact are related but should be assessed separately.

---

## Who Is Affected?

Determine whether the issue affects:

- One customer
- A group of customers
- A geographic region
- A product feature
- All customers

---

## What Is Affected?

Identify:

- Login
- Payments
- API
- Data access
- Notifications
- Reporting
- Application availability

---

## When Did It Start?

Establish:

- First known occurrence
- Detection time
- Customer reports
- Recovery time

---

## Business Impact

Consider:

- Revenue impact
- Customer productivity
- SLA impact
- Data integrity
- Security implications
- Regulatory implications

---

## Impact Statement

Use a concise statement such as:

> Customers using the reporting functionality experienced delayed report generation between 10:15 AM and 10:42 AM.

Avoid speculation.

---

## After Resolution

Document:

- Number of affected customers
- Duration
- Functionality affected
- Business impact
- Preventive actions
